## Booking assistance
Passengers can book station assistance through the app or by phone at least 24 hours before travel. Booking later is possible but cannot be guaranteed.

## Changing a booking
A booking can be changed in the app up to 2 hours before the assistance time. Changes closer than that must be made by phone.

## Cancelling a booking
A booking can be cancelled free of charge at any time before the assistance time. Cancelling does not affect the train ticket, which is handled by the train operator.

## Ramps and wheelchair access
Staff can bring a ramp to the train door at stations marked as staffed in the app. At unstaffed stations, assistance must be arranged with the train crew.

## Assistance not arriving
If staff do not arrive within 10 minutes of the booked time, the passenger should call the helpline. The case is then passed to the station manager.

## Lost items
Lost items are handled by the train operator, not by assistance staff. Passengers should contact the operator's lost property service.

## Refunds and compensation
Refund and compensation decisions are made by the train operator. Support agents cannot promise or approve them.
